Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Cho&Co Healthcare Analytics Portal.

In production the portal connects to your Hospital Information System (HIS), Patient Administration System (PAS) and theatre / ED systems. Data is modelled into a governed analytics database and refreshed daily (or in near-real-time where source feeds allow). This demo uses fabricated data for a representative private hospital group.
Descriptive reports what happened (occupancy, ALOS, activity). Diagnostic explains why (e.g. boarding driven by occupancy). Predictive forecasts what is likely next (census, surge, readmission risk). Prescriptive recommends what to do and quantifies the value — the scenario simulator is a live example.
Yes. The platform is built POPIA-aligned: identifiable data stays in your governed environment, dashboards use role-based access and row-level security, and analytics can run on de-identified or aggregated data where appropriate. We sign a data-processing agreement before any integration.
Yes. Every view can be filtered and permissioned by facility, discipline, ward, theatre or clinician. Executives see the group roll-up; unit managers see only their area.
Forecasts combine historical seasonality (including the SA winter respiratory surge) with recent trend. Readmission risk is scored from clinical and administrative features. Models are transparent, monitored for drift, and always presented with confidence ranges — never as black-box certainty.
A working pilot on one facility typically goes live within 4–6 weeks of data access, starting with the descriptive and diagnostic layers, then adding predictive and prescriptive as history accumulates. Email info@choandco.co.za to scope it.
